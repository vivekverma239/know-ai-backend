This report provides a detailed description of the "Economic Activity" chart.

### **Chart Overview**
*   **Title:** Economic Activity
*   **Units:** Percent change, year-over-year (y/y), based on a 3-month moving average (3m m.a.).
*   **Time Period:** November 2020 to March 2024.

### **Axes and Scales**
*   **X-Axis:** Time, with markers for November, March, and July across the years 2020 to 2024.
*   **Primary Y-Axis (Left):** Measures percentage change for the "Total" and "Ex. Agr" series. Ranges from -10 to 30.
*   **Secondary Y-Axis (Right - RHS):** Measures percentage change specifically for the "Agr" (Agriculture) series. Ranges from -40 to 40. Note: The 0 level on the primary axis corresponds to approximately -20 on the secondary axis.

### **Data Series Legend**
1.  **Total (Dark Blue Line):** Uses the left axis. Represents total economic activity.
2.  **Ex. Agr (Medium Blue Line):** "Excluding Agriculture," uses the left axis. Represents economic activity minus the agricultural sector.
3.  **Agr (RHS) (Light Blue Line):** "Agriculture," uses the right-hand side axis. Represents agricultural sector activity.

### **Key Observations**
*   **Total and Non-Agricultural Activity:** Both series peaked significantly in early-to-mid 2021 (reaching over 20%) before entering a long-term downward trend. They briefly returned to near 0% in late 2023 before contracting again in early 2024.
*   **Agricultural Volatility:** The agricultural sector (RHS) shows extreme fluctuations. It experienced a massive contraction in mid-2023 (reaching approx -35%) but has shown a strong recovery trend into 2024, diverging from the total economic trend.
