### Chart Description: Maturity and Yields of ARS (CER) Debt Issuances

This scatter plot illustrates the relationship between the maturity (in days) and the yields (in percent) for ARS (CER) debt issuances across two distinct time periods.

| Component | Details |

| **Title** | Maturity and Yields of ARS (CER) Debt Issuances |

#### Data Summary
The chart compares debt issuances from late 2023 against those from late 2023 to mid-2024.

| Data Series | Maturity Range (approx. days) | Yield Range (approx. %) | Observations |

*   **Outliers (Dec 23-May 24):** Two short-term issuances (approx. 100 days) recorded exceptionally low yields near **-88%** and **-110%**.

*   **Clustering (Sept-Nov 23):** The majority of these issuances are concentrated within the **100 to 450-day** maturity range, maintaining yields mostly in positive territory between **0% and +10%**.
